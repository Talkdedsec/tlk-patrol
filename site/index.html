00 · Briefing

Built in Godot 4.7 with Jolt physics. Every model, sound, siren and interior is original, generated from scripts. No ripped assets, no stock packs.

01 · Shift log

One shift,
start to finish.

  1. Roll call

    A procedural city with a road graph, traffic lights, day and night, weather and a full lighting pass. Walk out to the car and start your shift.

  2. On patrol

    Ten-plus original vehicles with real driving physics, opening doors, damage smoke and a follow camera. 12 to 92 mph in 7.9 seconds.

  3. Run it

    RADAR with lock, ALPR plate reader, minimap, gauges and an MDT terminal. Dispatch talks to you over a synthesized radio voice.

  4. Traffic stop

    Twenty-one dialogue options, citations and arrests. Every stop can go quiet or go sideways.

  5. Pursuit

    Request PIT, wait for authorization, call backup, or call it off. Foot chases end with a Taser and cuffs.

  6. Officer needs assistance

    Pistol, patrol rifle and shotgun with recoil, aiming and effects. Three weapons, one holster key.
